# Tadkhir

**Tadkhir** is an open-source, privacy-focused personal productivity application designed to bring everyday tasks, time, routines, notes, and personal finances into one simple and self-contained workspace.

It is built around a **local-first** approach: your data lives on your device and the application remains fully usable offline. When private synchronization is enabled, data is encrypted on the client before being sent to the server, so the server does not hold the keys required to read your data.

Tadkhir combines practical tools for everyday use without turning them into separate applications:

- **Tasks & time tracking** — create tasks, estimate work, run timed sessions, and review activity.
- **Routines** — manage recurring timed or counter-based activities and track daily progress.
- **Notes & pages** — keep simple free-form pages and useful information in one place.
- **Personal finance** — record income and expenses, organize categories, manage recurring transactions, and track debts and payments.
- **Offline-first workflow** — continue working without an internet connection and synchronize changes when connectivity returns.
- **Private synchronization** — synchronize data across devices through an encrypted private space.
- **Backup & portability** — export and restore personal data through encrypted files.
- **Responsive interface** — designed to work across desktop and mobile devices, with consistent components and support for both LTR and RTL interfaces.

Tadkhir deliberately avoids unnecessary complexity. It does not try to become a social network, a cloud service that owns your data, or a collection of disconnected tools. Its goal is simpler:

> **Give you one private place to organize what you need, track what you do, and keep control of your data.**

Tadkhir is open source because the application, its architecture, and its approach to privacy should be transparent, inspectable, and available for anyone to use, improve, or build upon.

**Local by default. Private by design. Open by choice.**
